# Battlechan SocialFi — Assessment & Notes

## Core Concept
- Controversial content platform on Solana
- Upvote/downvote based on Token-2022 with 50% burn + 50% team fee on downvotes
- PDA thread vaults with timers — posts auto-delete when timer hits 0
- Session keys + ZK compression ($0.002/user) + gas sponsorship

## Why It Could Still Work
- **Controversy = revenue**: Downvote tax model means controversial content literally prints money. Every hate-click = 50% burn + 50% to team wallet.
- **AI token market is hot**: Even if general crypto is bear, AI tokens (VVV, TAO, FET) are outperforming.
- **Unique mechanic**: No other SocialFi does "downvote to destroy" with burn mechanics.

## Why Timing Is Wrong (Now)
- General crypto bear market = bad time to launch a token project
- SocialFi is a graveyard — friend.tech, Farcaster, Lens all peaked and faded
- Capital needed for deployment + marketing + liquidity
- Would need to host dApp, pay gas sponsorship, fund liquidity pool

## Recommendation
- **File under "build when profitable"** — codebase knowledge (Privy + session keys + ZK compression) is valuable for future Solana work
- **Don't launch now** — focus on cash-flow products (CoinGecko, Telegram mod, NAGA-1)
- **Revisit when Node0 has recurring revenue** — then the $5-10k needed for launch is trivial

## Related: Node0 Token Idea
- Launch utility token (like VVV/Virtuals Protocol model)
- Stake tokens → get free NAGA-1 model access (API calls, inference hours)
- People buy tokens now → stake for 1-2 years access → gives Node0 cash upfront for equipment
- Bootstrapping without VC: presale → equipment → service → token value grows from usage
- Alternative: "Proof of Inference" — stake tokens, get inference credits proportional to stake
